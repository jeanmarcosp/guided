## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Guided turns the places you're planning around — a trip, a neighborhood,
a weekend — into a curated map guide instead of a scattered list of
screenshots and texts.

Search for a spot, add it to a guide, and it's automatically sorted into
a matching layer: food, coffee, shopping, and more. See everything on a
map or scan it as a list, filtered by layer.

FEATURES

• Guides — create, color, and organize collections of places
• Layers — places auto-sort by category; rename, recolor, hide, or
  reorder layers to fit how you actually think about a trip
• Map + list views — see every pin on a map, or browse as a list
• Smart place search — finds real places as you type
• Share & collaborate — invite people to view or edit a guide together;
  changes sync live, so everyone's always looking at the same plan
• Personal "visited" marks — quietly track your own progress through a
  guide without changing what collaborators see
• Works offline — guides you've opened stay usable without a connection,
  and sync again once you're back online
• Light, dark, or system appearance

Whether you're planning a trip with friends, keeping a running list of
places to try in your own city, or just tired of losing good
recommendations in a group chat, Guided keeps it organized — and keeps
everyone on the same page.
```

(~1,050 characters — well under the limit; room to expand with a
screenshots-driven narrative once those exist, or leave it concise.)
